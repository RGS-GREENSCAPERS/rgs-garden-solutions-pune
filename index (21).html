Our Story

Started with one farmhouse and a lot of stubborn soil

RGS Landscapers began when Sandip started helping a relative maintain a farmhouse garden in Bhugaon that nobody else wanted to take on — poor soil, patchy lawn, half-dead trees. Getting it right taught him that most garden problems in this belt come down to the same three things: soil, sourcing, and someone actually showing up on time.

That's the whole idea behind the business. We don't just plant and leave — we supply our own soil and vermicompost, pick plants suited to Mulshi's climate, and stay on for maintenance instead of disappearing after the first job.

Today the team handles everything from small balcony setups to full farmhouse landscapes and society gardens across the area, still run the same hands-on way it started.
